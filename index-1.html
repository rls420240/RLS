About Roberto

Roberto's Latin Sound is a professional live music entertainment service with over 20 years of musical experience. Roberto began his career as a composer in Uruguay and Argentina and later continued his work in Canada, performing across Toronto and surrounding areas.

He has written more than 150 original songs, released three CDs in English and Spanish, and hosted the TV show Latin Life. A multiple time talent competition winner, Roberto is passionate about live performance and creating joyful, high energy musical experiences for every audience.
